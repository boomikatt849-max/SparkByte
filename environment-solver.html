3. Impact Assessment

Explore how environmental problems can affect different areas of society.

🌱 Biodiversity

Habitat destruction and pollution can reduce species populations and disturb food webs.

🏙️ Human Communities

Environmental degradation can affect public health, livelihoods and access to clean resources.

🌡️ Climate

Deforestation and greenhouse gas emissions contribute to changes in the climate system.

💰 Economy

Environmental damage can affect agriculture, fisheries, infrastructure and local industries.
