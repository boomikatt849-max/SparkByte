Ecosystem Simulator

Explore ecological relationships, population changes and environmental impacts.

1. Ecosystem Explorer

An ecosystem is a community of living organisms interacting with one another and with their physical environment.

🌳 Forest Ecosystem

Contains trees, animals, fungi and microorganisms. Trees provide food and shelter.

🌾 Grassland Ecosystem

Dominated by grasses, grazing animals and predators. Rainfall is usually seasonal.

🏜️ Desert Ecosystem

Receives very little rainfall. Organisms have adaptations to conserve water.

🌊 Aquatic Ecosystem

Includes freshwater and marine environments with fish, algae and aquatic plants.

2. Food Web Simulator

A food web shows how energy moves between organisms in an ecosystem.

🌱 Producers

Plants and algae make their own food using sunlight.

🐇 Primary Consumers

Herbivores obtain energy by eating producers.

🐍 Secondary Consumers

These organisms feed on primary consumers.

🍄 Decomposers

Fungi and bacteria break down dead organic matter and recycle nutrients.

4. Environmental Impact Lab

Explore how environmental changes can affect ecosystems.

🌡️ Rising Temperature

Can change habitats, breeding seasons and species distribution.

🌲 Deforestation

Reduces habitats, affects biodiversity and can increase soil erosion.

💧 Water Pollution

Can reduce water quality and harm aquatic organisms.
