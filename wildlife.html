Wildlife Research Overview

Biodiversity refers to the variety of life found on Earth, including animals, plants and microorganisms. Wildlife plays an important role in maintaining ecosystem balance through food chains, pollination, seed dispersal and nutrient cycling.

Animal Classification

Animals are classified into major groups based on their body structure, characteristics and biological features.


Mammals: Have hair or fur and females generally produce milk.

Birds: Have feathers, beaks and lay eggs.

Reptiles: Usually have dry, scaly skin.

Amphibians: Commonly have life stages associated with both water and land.

Fish: Aquatic vertebrates that generally breathe using gills.
