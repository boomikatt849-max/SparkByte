Research Overview

Climate science studies long-term patterns in Earth's atmosphere, oceans, land and ice systems. Human activities such as fossil fuel combustion and deforestation increase greenhouse gas concentrations, contributing to global warming and changes in climate patterns.

Understanding the Greenhouse Effect

The greenhouse effect is a natural process that keeps Earth warm. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit some of the infrared radiation released by Earth's surface. Human activities increase the concentration of these gases, strengthening this effect.

☀️ 01. Solar Energy

Sunlight passes through the atmosphere and reaches Earth's surface.

🌎 02. Absorption

Land and oceans absorb energy and become warmer.

♨️ 03. Heat Release

Earth releases energy back toward space as infrared radiation.

☁️ 04. Greenhouse Gases

Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit some outgoing infrared radiation.

Climate Change Case Studies

🌊 Sea Level Rise

Warming oceans expand, while melting land-based ice adds water to the oceans. These processes contribute to rising global sea levels.

🌡️ Heatwaves

Human-caused warming increases the likelihood and intensity of many extreme heat events, affecting health and ecosystems.

🌳 Deforestation

Forest loss reduces carbon storage and can alter local rainfall, soil conditions and biodiversity.

🌧️ Rainfall Changes

A warming atmosphere changes the water cycle and can influence heavy rainfall and drought patterns.
